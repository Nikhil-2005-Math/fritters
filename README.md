## 🌟 What's New & Upgraded

1. **Native Malayalam Identity (പഴംപൊരി)**:
   - Name prominently featured in authentic Malayalam: **പഴംപൊരി** across the navigation, header, cart, and covers.

2. **Founder Profile: Dr. Aleena Jacob**:
   - Executive profile for Founder & CEO **Dr. Aleena Jacob**.
   - **Interactive Photo Space**: Includes a styled frame with a "📷 Upload Photo" button that allows you to upload any image from your computer. The uploaded photo preview is permanently saved to your browser (`localStorage`).
   - Official founder statement and Ph.D. credentials in frying thermodynamics.

3. **Standard & Premium Pazhampori Varieties**:
   - Replaced all shawarma mentions with 9 distinct varieties:
   - **Standard Pazhampori (സ്റ്റാൻഡേർഡ്)**:
     - *Classic Kerala Pazhampori* (Traditional coconut oil fry)
     - *Crispy Street-Cut Fritter* (78 dB crunch tea-stall cut)
     - *Cardamom Infused Pazhampori* (Idukki green cardamom aroma)
     - *Zero-Sugar Organic Fritter* (100% natural fruit sweetness)
   - **Premium Pazhampori (പ്രീമിയം)**:
     - *Royal Cow-Ghee Pazhampori* (Fried in pure Kasaragod cow ghee, saffron glaze)
     - *24K Gold Leaf & Forest Honey Reserve* (Edible gold leaf, Wayanad wild honey, almonds)
     - *Jaggery & Roasted Coconut Stuffed Fritter* (Marayoor jaggery core)
     - *Kozhikode Halwa Layered Fritter* (Warm black halwa sandwich)
     - *Malabar Spicy Beef-Roast Stuffed Pazhampori* (Sweet & savory legend)

4. **Branded Eco-Cover Packaging (Logo & Name Aligned)**:
   - Custom food-grade kraft paper pouch with micro-ventilation slits.
   - Beautifully aligned circular emblem with the Malayalam name **പഴംപൊരി**, English subtitle, and *Dr. Aleena Jacob Foundation* certification seal.
   - Dedicated packaging gallery to inspect the design.

5. **Dynamic Gate Delivery Tracker & Cancel Lockout**:
   - **Cancel Allowed**: While the delivery is frying, packaging, and in transit (> 80m away).
   - **Cancel Locked Out**: As soon as the delivery boy arrives within 50 meters of your gate (`cust-gate`), the Cancel button is **automatically locked & disabled** with a notice:
     *“CANCELLATION STRICTLY PROHIBITED: The delivery boy is standing at your gate holding steaming hot Pazhampori! Refusing fresh Pazhampori at your gate is forbidden!”*

6. **Persistent Order History**:
   - Every placed order is saved to `localStorage`.
   - Click **"📋 ഓർഡർ ചരിത്രം"** in the top bar to inspect past orders, totals, item breakdowns, and re-track active deliveries anytime.

7. **Multi-Item Shopping Cart**:
   - Add multiple standard and premium varieties with quantity controls (`-` / `+`).
   - Specify customer name, phone number, address, and specific gate/landmark.
